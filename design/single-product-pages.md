# Pearl and Ruby landing pages
Public routes: /pearl/ and /ruby/. Source: dist/pearl/index.html, dist/ruby/index.html. Shared order, config, tracking and receipt scripts. New editorial themes, product-specific copy and social cards. Price and volume match the existing v2 receiver (50 ml), rather than the conflicting 70 ml title in the provided Ladi export. Each single-page checkout offers one bottle and links to the mixed duo. The same-product two-bottle offer in Ladi is not part of receiver v2.
Both pages redirect after the validated ACK to ../ruby-pearl/cam-on.html; customer data remains in sessionStorage. The current Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and replay settings are reused. No additional Pixel IDs from the Ladi export are enabled. Form and dialog retain replay privacy markers. No tracking scripts on the receipt page. Preview mode is preserved; Apps Script v2 must be deployed before real orders can be accepted. No artificial countdown, unverified testimonial or unverified video copied.
Run node scripts/sync-pages.mjs after changes. Test with node tests/single-product-tests.js and existing frontend, receipt and tracking tests.
